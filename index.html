Who is EvilJay?

EvilJayTheDevil, also known as EvilJay and Jay Frazier, is a comedian, podcast host, streamer and music artist. He was born in Chicago and lives in Minneapolis and Las Vegas.

He hosts the EvilSinsTV Podcast: savage comedy, chaos and real talk, with interviews, live content and unfiltered conversation.
